# IDD-0002.spec-toc-and-structural-discovery

## Intent

Recover book structure from extracted text, using an existing table of contents as a strong reference when available and supporting books that lack one.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001
- IDD-0003
- IDD-0004

## Behavior

- The application looks for a table of contents near the start of the document using deterministic signals such as contents labels, page numbers, numbering patterns, repeated short lines, and chapter/part conventions.
- Deterministic TOC parsing and unambiguous TOC/body matching remain preferred where they are reliable.
- Generic streaming heading discovery is different: the semantic candidate detector receives the complete decision-zone text, including ordinary body paragraphs. Deterministic candidate filtering is not a mandatory gate before that model call.
- The detector proposes only local decision-zone numbers. It does not assign heading levels or create annotations.
- Each proposed candidate is independently verified by a second semantic operation. Only an accepted verifier result with level 1 through 3 can become a new heading annotation.
- Existing deterministically confirmed Heading, Noise, TOC-related, or other structural annotations remain authoritative. They stay visible as read-only context and are not reclassified by the generic heading scan.
- Deterministic noise detection, page-number detection, repeated header/footer detection, TOC processing, structural validation, and hierarchy consistency checks remain application responsibilities.
- Introduction, preface, appendix, references, and comparable parts receive level 1 when their structural rank equals a chapter.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Semantic heading discovery may inspect ordinary body text because deciding whether a line is structurally significant is a semantic task. Pointer identity, ownership, eligibility, annotations, hierarchy, and commit remain application-owned.

## Non-goals

- Treating a table of contents as a replacement source for body headings.
- Requiring deterministic heading-shape heuristics to approve a paragraph before semantic heading discovery can see it.
- Marking every block with an explicit body annotation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Exact and punctuation-different TOC entries can be matched to the correct body pointers without inserting TOC titles into the body.
- A printed page number cannot become body-heading text.
- Repeated running headers are not promoted to chapters.
- Generic heading discovery receives every decision-zone paragraph, while only eligible paragraphs may be selected.
- Deterministically confirmed headings do not require semantic verification and are not overwritten.

## Verification

- Verify books with exact and punctuation-different TOCs, page numbers, repeated headers, appendices, and no TOC.
- Verify that ordinary body paragraphs are present in candidate-detector input.
- Verify that a confirmed structural annotation cannot be selected or replaced by the generic heading scan.
